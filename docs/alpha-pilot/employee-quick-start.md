# Employee Quick Start

This guide is for employees invited into a WorkMap alpha workspace.

## What WorkMap Is

WorkMap is a transparent virtual office and workplace visibility tool for a small pilot team. It shows your avatar, office presence, basic workspace status, and limited activity summaries when the alpha harness/scaffold is used.

WorkMap is not hidden surveillance. The alpha does not collect screenshots, keystrokes, clipboard content, webcam/microphone data, private message or email content, passwords, form inputs, or full webpage content.

## Accept an Invite

1. Open the invite link from your Owner.
2. Sign in or sign up with Cognito using the invited email address.
3. Return to the invite page if the app sends you through login.
4. Enter a human-readable display name when asked.
5. Accept the invitation.

The backend checks that your verified Cognito email matches the invite. If you sign in with the wrong email, acceptance should fail safely.

## Complete First-Time Setup

After accepting the invite, complete the required setup flow:

1. Review the transparency/compliance page.
2. Create or confirm your avatar/profile.
3. Complete device setup guidance if shown.
4. Enter the virtual office.

Your avatar/profile is saved to the backend. On later logins or a fresh browser session, you should not need to recreate the avatar if the backend profile is complete.

## Use the Virtual Office

- Move with WASD or arrow keys.
- Double-click a walkable location to auto-walk.
- Press E near supported chairs to sit or stand.
- Use the People panel to see teammates in your workspace.
- Open teammate details through the People panel or contact drawer.

Your teammates may see your avatar location, workspace status, and last-seen freshness. They do not see screenshots, private content, keystrokes, camera/mic data, or message bodies.

## Reports

Employee reports are scoped to your own activity summaries where scaffold data exists. Company-wide aggregate summaries are for Owner/Manager-style roles and should be blocked for Employee accounts.

If your reports are empty, it may simply mean no device/app/domain sample activity has been submitted yet.

## Compliance

Read the compliance page before pilot activity begins. It explains what WorkMap collects, what it does not collect, and why the data exists.

## What Your Owner Can See

Your Owner may see:

- Your directory entry and display name.
- Your virtual office avatar, location, status, and last-seen freshness.
- Aggregate company-level app/domain summaries when scaffold activity exists.
- Device coverage and compliance acknowledgement status where available.

Your Owner should not see:

- Screenshots or recordings.
- Keystrokes, clipboard, webcam, microphone, or private content.
- Private messages or email bodies.
- Passwords or form inputs.
- Full URL paths, queries, fragments, page content, or raw browsing history.
