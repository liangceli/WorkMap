CandidGrid Domain Tracking

Under the current workspace policy, CandidGrid can record the focused HTTP/HTTPS hostname and whether trusted keyboard, mouse, wheel or touch activity was recently observed to classify Focus as active or focused idle. If enabled by policy, it can separately record Domain open/runtime while at least one eligible tab for a hostname remains open; same-host tabs are de-duplicated, different hostnames may overlap, and runtime is not Focus or work time. Broad site permission is used to observe eligible hostnames, while configured sensitive hostnames are discarded on this device before queueing. CandidGrid never records key values, typed text, pointer coordinates, scroll direction, target elements, full URL paths, queries or fragments, page titles or content, form input, passwords, screenshots or recordings, clipboard, camera, microphone, external email bodies or private messages. Pairing does not itself authorise collection: the current policy, notice confirmation, permission and schedule must also allow it. Read this product notice with your organisation's workplace monitoring or privacy notice; confirming receipt is not consent or a waiver of rights.
